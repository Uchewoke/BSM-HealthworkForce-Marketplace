# 03 — Revenue Model

BSM uses a **multi-revenue model** so it does not depend on a single source of income.

## All Revenue Streams

| Revenue stream | How it works |
|---|---|
| Facility subscriptions | Facilities pay monthly or annually for platform access. |
| Agency subscriptions | Staffing agencies pay for candidate management, placement coordination and client tools. |
| Placement fees | BSM charges a fee when a direct-hire placement is completed. |
| Temporary staffing fees | BSM earns a disclosed platform fee or contractual margin on eligible staffing transactions. |
| Enterprise services | Larger healthcare organizations pay for integrations, advanced reporting and customized workflows. |

## Launch Priorities (Facility-First)

| Revenue model | Target customer | Payment structure |
|---|---|---|
| Facility subscription | Nursing homes, clinics and other healthcare providers | Monthly or annual subscription |
| Direct-hire placement fee | Facilities hiring permanent employees | Fee per successful placement |
| Temporary staffing transaction fee | Facilities using temporary staffing | Contractually defined fee or margin |

Agency subscriptions and enterprise integrations come later, as the marketplace grows.

## Pricing Principles

- Pricing is **configurable**, so commercial models can be tested without rebuilding the application.
- Final pricing should be validated through customer interviews and pilot contracts, not assumed in advance.
