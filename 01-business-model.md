# 01 — Business Model: Hybrid Healthcare Staffing Marketplace

**Selected model: Option C, Hybrid Healthcare Staffing Marketplace.**

It is the strongest fit because one platform handles direct hiring, temporary shift staffing and staffing agency partnerships.

## Three Staffing Channels

1. **Direct hiring.** Healthcare facilities recruit and hire healthcare professionals directly.
2. **Temporary and per-diem staffing.** Facilities post shifts that qualified professionals can accept.
3. **Staffing agency partnerships.** Agencies supply candidates to healthcare facilities and manage their own workforce through the platform.

## How the Hybrid Model Works

| Feature | Healthcare Facilities | Healthcare Professionals | Staffing Agencies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Create an account | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Post jobs and shifts | Yes | No | Yes, subject to permissions |
| Search for candidates | Yes | No | Yes |
| Apply for jobs and shifts | No | Yes | Submit candidates |
| Manage credentials | Review and verify | Upload and maintain | Manage candidate records |
| Manage placements | Yes | View assignments | Yes |
| Manage timesheets | Approve | Submit | Review or approve, as configured |
| Billing and payments | Pay applicable fees | View applicable earnings | Manage agency billing |
| Analytics | Workforce analytics | Personal activity | Placement and revenue analytics |

> Access to each feature depends on the user's role, organization, contract and permissions.

## Operating Model

BSM HealthForce Marketplace operates as a **technology marketplace only**. BSM supplies the platform. Facilities, professionals and agencies are the parties to the staffing relationships, and BSM does not employ every healthcare professional directly.
