| 21 | Applying is refused for `ineligible`, `expired_credential` and `unavailable` outcomes but allowed for `pending_verification` and `needs_review` | Lets professionals apply while credentials are under review without implying eligibility | Facilities see applicants who cannot be hired yet; or good candidates are blocked by a rule | Confirm the apply gate | 2026-10-30 open |
| 22 | A credential verified by document review counts as "met" for eligibility, with its method shown | No decision yet on whether facilities require primary-source verification (#17) | Facilities hire on credentials that were never checked at the source | Decide per facility/credential whether primary-source is required | 2026-10-30 open |
| 23 | Specialty mismatch, experience shortfall (self-reported), other-state licenses and free-text requirements give `needs_review`, not ineligible; compact privileges are not evaluated | Avoid automatic exclusion on unverified or legally nuanced facts | Reviewers carry more manual work; or a nuanced case is mishandled | Legal/domain review of eligibility rules and compact handling | 2026-10-30 open |
| 24 | Ranking factors and default weights (25/20/15/15/10/10/5) are the blueprint's proposal, not validated | Required explainability, no benchmark | Biased or poor ordering | Domain + employment-law review before any pilot | 2026-10-30 open |
| 25 | `accepted` is a terminal status that records acceptance only; no assignment, pay terms or employment/contractor relationship is created | Those are Release 3 and legal-dependent | Users read "accepted" as a binding engagement | Define offer/assignment terms and legal relationship | 2026-10-30 open |
| 26 | Email notifications are best-effort with no retry queue, and contain only a short title | No provider or job queue selected | Missed email notices | Select email provider; add a retry-safe worker | 2026-10-30 open |

| 28 | Platform admins (not only super admins) may pause/close any job and suspend non-platform users; only super admins may suspend platform users or view metrics/ranking weights; platform admins cannot read the audit log (auditor and super admin can) | Least privilege; no spec | Too much or too little staff power; duty-separation gaps | Confirm the platform role matrix | 2026-10-30 open |
