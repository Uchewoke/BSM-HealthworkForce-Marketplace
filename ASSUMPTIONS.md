| 20 | A published job's requirements, profession, location and employment type are frozen (pause/close and create a new job to change them) | Avoid misleading applicants and eligibility drift | Friction for facilities | Confirm or allow versioned edits | 2026-10-30 open |
| 21 | Applying is refused for `ineligible`, `expired_credential` and `unavailable` outcomes but allowed for `pending_verification` and `needs_review` | Lets professionals apply while credentials are under review without implying eligibility | Facilities see applicants who cannot be hired yet; or good candidates are blocked by a rule | Confirm the apply gate | 2026-10-30 open |
| 22 | A credential verified by document review counts as "met" for eligibility, with its method shown | No decision yet on whether facilities require primary-source verification (#17) | Facilities hire on credentials that were never checked at the source | Decide per facility/credential whether primary-source is required | 2026-10-30 open |
| 23 | Specialty mismatch, experience shortfall (self-reported), other-state licenses and free-text requirements give `needs_review`, not ineligible; compact privileges are not evaluated | Avoid automatic exclusion on unverified or legally nuanced facts | Reviewers carry more manual work; or a nuanced case is mishandled | Legal/domain review of eligibility rules and compact handling | 2026-10-30 open |
| 24 | Ranking factors and default weights (25/20/15/15/10/10/5) are the blueprint's proposal, not validated | Required explainability, no benchmark | Biased or poor ordering | Domain + employment-law review before any pilot | 2026-10-30 open |
| 25 | `accepted` is a terminal status that records acceptance only; no assignment, pay terms or employment/contractor relationship is created | Those are Release 3 and legal-dependent | Users read "accepted" as a binding engagement | Define offer/assignment terms and legal relationship | 2026-10-30 open |
| 26 | Email notifications are best-effort with no retry queue, and contain only a short title | No provider or job queue selected | Missed email notices | Select email provider; add a retry-safe worker | 2026-10-30 open |
| 27 | All US states are enabled by default; platform admins may disable states (hides jobs, blocks publishing there). | 28 | Platform admins (not only super admins) may pause/close any job and suspend non-platform users; only super admins may suspend platform users or view metrics/ranking weights; platform admins cannot read the audit log (auditor and super admin can) | Least privilege; no spec | Too much or too little staff power; duty-separation gaps | Confirm the platform role matrix | 2026-10-30 open |
| 29 | Disputes are a ticket category, not a separate adjudication workflow; no refund/cancellation policy exists | Release 3 has payments; policy undecided (#10) | Disputes need binding outcomes later | Define dispute and cancellation policies | 2026-10-30 open |
| 30 | Row-level security is deferred until it can be built and tested on real PostgreSQL (ADR 0004) | Unverifiable on the embedded test database | A missed tenant filter in future code is not caught by the database | Schedule RLS work before any pilot | 2026-10-30 open |
| 31 | **Decided 2026-10-30 by the product owner:** facilities search an anonymized card of opt-in professionals and can only invite them to apply; identity is revealed on application (ADR 0005) | Resolves the open facility-search privacy question; extends #6 and #15 | Re-identification from combined fields; unsettled legal treatment | Counsel to review notices, consent wording and the "sale/sharing" question before pilot | 2026-10-30 decided (legal review open) |
| 32 | The web and admin UIs hide controls a role cannot use, but the server is the only authority. | 33 | UI copy (landing, how it works, consent and visibility text) is a first draft and has not had legal, accessibility-specialist or plain-language review | No reviewer available | Misleading or non-compliant wording | Legal and accessibility review of all user-facing text | 2026-10-30 open |
| 34 | Shift offers expire after 48 hours by default (never later than the shift start); a professional may cancel a confirmed assignment any time before the shift starts, with a required reason and no penalty; facilities likewise. Assignment status only records bookings: pay and terms are agreed directly, no payment is processed | Cancellation, no-show and dispute policy undecided (#10) | Late cancellations harm facilities; no consequences for repeat cancellers | Decide cancellation window, penalties and dispute handling | open (defaults applied) |
| 35 | Shift offers and assignments reuse `applications.read`/`applications.manage`; schedulers cannot send offers. Platform admin oversight of offers and assignments is not built | Avoids permission changes in this release | Roles too coarse | Decide whether a scheduling-specific permission is needed | open |
| 11 | Matching weights (25/20/15/15/10/10/5) | Blueprint proposal, not validated | Biased or poor ranking | Domain + counsel review | open |
| 36 | Agencies represent only professionals who accept a roster invitation; a professional can leave at any time. An agency submission is a normal application and goes through the same eligibility gate. Agencies see only jobs a facility explicitly grants them and never see credential details | Consent and least disclosure; reuses the Release 1 pipeline | Professionals dislike being represented; a professional may be submitted by an agency and also apply directly (second attempt is a conflict) | Product and counsel to confirm consent wording, exclusivity rules and who "owns" a candidate | open |
| 37 | Agency review reuses the `admin.facility_review` permission and the facility review state machine; agency fees, markups, rate cards and billing are not modelled (placements show the offer rate only) | Payments are a later Phase 2 module (manual invoicing chosen) | Agency commercial terms are handled off-platform | Define agency commercial terms with the billing module | open |
| 38 | Agencies cannot invite professionals who have no account; the invite endpoint answers identically either way so it cannot be used to discover accounts | Privacy; avoids user enumeration | Agencies must ask candidates to register first | Decide whether to support email invitations that create accounts | open |
| 39 | BSM bills the organization that owns the job a percentage fee on each completed assignment, based on the agreed hourly rate and the scheduled shift length. The fee rate is intentionally not defaulted; payment is recorded by hand after being received outside the platform | Manual invoicing chosen 2026-11-01; revenue model (03-revenue-model.md) not finalised | Wrong fee basis (subscriptions, placement fees, actual hours) is billed; BSM could be seen as handling payments | Decide the fee model with finance/counsel; add timesheets before billing actual hours | open |
| 40 | Invoices are USD only, untaxed, with no credit notes, partial payments or dunning. Voided invoices keep their number and lines for the audit trail; paid invoices are final | Smallest honest manual workflow | Corrections need a void and re-issue; tax obligations unmet | Confirm tax and accounting requirements | open |
| 41 | Agencies are not billed or invoiced yet, and agency commercial terms with facilities are off-platform | Agency billing model undecided (#37) | Agencies expect platform invoicing | Define the agency subscription and fee model | open |
| 42 | Timesheets are self-reported by the professional and approved or returned by the facility; there is no clock-in, geolocation or third-party verification. A facility cannot edit hours, only return them. Approval is final | Smallest workable flow; avoids a facility silently changing what a professional is paid for | Disputes over hours have no adjustment path; self-reported hours can be wrong or inflated | Define a dispute and adjustment process (ties to #10) | open |
| 43 | Billed hours are net worked time (end - start - unpaid break), with no overtime, rounding, minimum-shift or rate rules. The 6 h early / 12 h late tolerance around the scheduled shift is a guess | No policy exists | Overpay or underpay versus real agreements; legitimate long shifts rejected | Confirm rounding, overtime and tolerance rules with facilities and counsel | open |
| 44 | Reviews are limited to verified work (completed assignment, approved timesheet), one per side, within 14 days, double-blind, uneditable. Professionals' reviews are anonymous to facilities. Disputes can only leave a review standing or remove it | Fairness and anti-retaliation design with no product spec | A 14-day window or anonymity may not suit facilities; removed reviews lose information; defamation and employment-law exposure from published comments | Counsel to review review/dispute policy, anonymity and retention before launch | open |
| 45 | Reliability figures (no-shows, cancellations, 48-hour late-cancel rule, completion rate) are shown to facilities on applications as context, with the sample size, and are never used in eligibility or ranking. | 46 | Analytics use UTC calendar days, shift start for fill/demand, worked-end for hours and cost, and assignment creation for time to fill and funnel. "Agreed pay" is approved hours x the offer rate, an estimate because pay is settled directly | No reporting spec; simplest consistent definitions | Numbers differ from a facility's own books or local-time expectations; a rate change after the offer is not reflected | Agree metric definitions and time zone handling with pilot facilities | open |
| 47 | Cost figures are visible only to roles with billing.read; fill and outcome figures to anyone with jobs.read. Agencies see only their own submission and placement figures | Least privilege; cost is commercially sensitive | Hiring managers may expect cost visibility | Confirm who should see cost | open |
| 48 | The assistant sends the user's question and the aggregate figures its tools return to Anthropic's Claude API. Anthropic retention and data-processing terms are not yet reviewed against BSM's privacy notice, healthcare-adjacent data rules or customer contracts | Needed to provide the feature; no counsel review yet | Personal or business data sent to a third party without adequate notice or agreement | Counsel and security review of the data flow, provider terms (including retention) and the privacy notice before enabling in production | open |
| 49 | The assistant is advisory and read-only; the only artifact it produces is a draft job a person must save. Answers may be wrong, so the UI labels them AI-generated and tells users to verify | Keeps humans accountable and the rule engine authoritative | Users over-trust figures or drafts; a prompt-injection in user-written text steers answers | Evaluate accuracy and injection resistance with real model runs before launch; consider showing sources | open |
| 50 | Usage is limited only by 30 requests per user per hour and a 6-round tool loop. There is no per-organization spend cap, usage dashboard or alerting, and answers are not cached | Smallest safe control; real usage unknown | Unexpected API cost | Add org-level quotas, usage reporting and spend alerts once real usage exists | open |
