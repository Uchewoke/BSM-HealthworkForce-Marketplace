# 07 — Development Roadmap

## Phase 1: Facility Staffing MVP
Goal: build the minimum product needed to test whether facilities will use and pay for the service.

- [ ] Facility registration and organization management
- [ ] Facility profiles and locations
- [ ] Job and shift creation
- [ ] Healthcare professional profiles and registration
- [ ] Search and basic matching
- [ ] Applications and candidate submissions
- [ ] Initial credential-document management
- [ ] Notifications
- [ ] Administrative oversight and audit logs

## Phase 2: Staffing Operations
- [ ] Credential verification workflows
- [ ] Staffing agency portal
- [ ] Shift offers and assignment confirmation
- [ ] Availability and scheduling conflict checks
- [ ] Timesheets
- [ ] Invoicing and billing
- [ ] Facility staffing analytics

## Phase 3: Intelligent Workforce Marketplace
- [ ] AI-assisted candidate search
- [ ] Automated recommendations
- [ ] Staffing demand forecasting
- [ ] Advanced agency and facility reporting
- [ ] Payroll, credentialing, scheduling and other integrations, where commercially justified
