# 02 — The Five Core Components

## 1. Healthcare Workforce Marketplace
A searchable marketplace covering physicians, nurses, allied health professionals, therapists, medical technicians, healthcare assistants and other healthcare occupations.

## 2. Intelligent Matching Engine
Matches professionals to jobs on specialty, qualifications, location, availability, experience, compensation and shift requirements.

- A placement cannot be confirmed until the required credentials and eligibility checks are satisfied.

## 3. Credentialing and Compliance Center
Tracks licenses, certifications, expirations, verification status and required documentation.

- Verification requirements vary by profession, jurisdiction and facility.

## 4. Staffing Operations Engine
Manages applications, candidate submissions, shift offers, assignments, cancellations, timesheets and placement workflows.

## 5. Commercial and Billing Engine
Supports facility subscriptions, staffing agency agreements, direct-hire placement fees, temporary staffing fees, invoices and configurable payment arrangements.
