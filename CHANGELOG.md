# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Unfilled shift recovery and one-tap acceptance (ADR 0007; migration `0014`: `shift_recovery_settings`, `shift_recovery`, `origin` on `shift_offers` and `float_requests`, `assignments.undone_at`). **Recovery:** off until an owner or admin switches it on (`PUT /organizations/:orgId/shift-recovery/settings`, org.update); automatic offers are made and audited on behalf of the person who switched it on, and pause if they leave. When an offer or float request for a shift is declined or expires and the shift is still short, a pass (every `SHIFT_RECOVERY_INTERVAL_MINUTES`, default 5, or "Check now" via `POST …/shift-recovery/run`) offers it to the next best currently-eligible applicants, then to eligible float pool members (still anonymous), within the facility's limits (default 5 automatic offers per shift, 12 h offers, no new offers within 2 h of the start); anyone already asked is never asked again; eligibility is never relaxed and nothing is sent without a rate. Once per shift it escalates to the facility (notification plus `GET …/shift-recovery`) with the shortage, who was asked and how it went, why outreach stopped, and suggested next steps; facilities can start or stop it per shift (`…/shifts/:shiftId/start|stop`, and a "Keep trying to fill" button on the job's shifts). Float request creation moved into a shared service so the facility button and recovery obey the same rules. UI: facility Shift recovery page, overview alert, job shift button. **One-tap acceptance:** the professional's offers are now cards showing facility and address, times, rate, expiry, required credentials (mandatory and preferred), the message and shift notes, and whether they qualify right now (Accept is disabled with reasons if not); Accept is a single tap and returns what happens next; `POST /professionals/me/assignments/:id/undo` takes an acceptance back within 5 minutes with no reason, reopening the shift and not counting against reliability or cancellation statistics; larger touch targets and stacked buttons on narrow screens. Tests: `shift-recovery.test.ts` (16), `one-tap.test.ts` (6); the time-of-day dependence in test shifts is avoided. **Not built:** quiet hours, push notifications or a mobile app, onboarding paperwork after acceptance, recovery for talent-search candidates, a worker process separate from the API. **Not verified:** the new pages have not been opened in a browser or on a phone, and the scheduler has not run against a real database over time.
- Phase 3, demand forecasting and the regional float pool (ADR 0006). **Forecast** (no migration): `GET /organizations/:orgId/forecast?horizon=4..12` (jobs.read) returns a weekly demand forecast overall and for the five busiest professions: expected positions with a low-high range, a confidence label, the method, positions already posted (a floor), an estimate of what may go unfilled from the recent fill rate, and a walk-forward back-test of how wrong the method has been. Method: recency-weighted average with a damped, clipped trend; no seasonality. With less than 8 weeks or 10 positions of history it says `insufficient_history` instead of guessing. The assistant gained a read-only `get_demand_forecast` tool. **Float pool** (migration `0013`: `exchange_regions`, `float_pool_members`, `exchange_participants`, `float_requests`, `applications.source`): staff create regions (named sets of states) under `/admin/exchange` (admin.marketplace_oversight), and can suspend a facility; professionals opt in per region with versioned consent (`/professionals/me/float-pool`); facilities agree to versioned exchange terms (org.update) and then see anonymous pool cards (`/organizations/:orgId/exchange/pool`, matched to one open shift with the normal eligibility engine) and send shift requests. A request is not an application: the person stays anonymous until they accept; accepting runs pre-flight checks, creates the application (`source = float_pool`), and books through the existing `createShiftOffer` and `respondToShiftOffer`, so eligibility, scheduling conflicts and capacity still apply. Leaving, withdrawal and suspension withdraw waiting requests; requests expire after 48 h or at shift start. Small pool counts are hidden (fewer than 5). UI: facility Forecast and Regional exchange pages (forecast shows pool capacity beside demand), professional Regional float pool page, admin Regional exchange page; a ForecastChart in the chart kit. Tests: `forecast-model.test.ts`, `forecast.test.ts`, `exchange.test.ts`, and chart tests. **Not built:** seasonality, holiday or event effects, sub-state regions, cross-facility shift sharing boards, pooled credential verification, pay benchmarks, automatic requests, an exchange assistant. **Not verified:** pages have not been opened in a browser; the consent and exchange terms are unreviewed drafts.
- Phase 2, AI staffing assistant (no migration). A read-only chat assistant for facility staff (`POST /organizations/:orgId/assistant`, `GET …/assistant/status`; jobs.read, approved organizations) and agency staff (`…/agency/assistant`; agency.jobs.read). It calls Claude through the official Anthropic SDK (`backend/src/integrations/assistant.ts`; default model `claude-opus-5-5`, override with `ASSISTANT_MODEL`, effort with `ASSISTANT_EFFORT`, server-side refusal fallback on) in a manual tool loop capped at 6 rounds. **It cannot change anything.** Its tools are server-side, read-only and bound to the caller's own organization (the model never supplies an organization id): staffing summary (the analytics figures, with cost only for roles with billing.read), pending work, job list, job setup options, and `propose_job_draft`, which validates a posting against the organization's real locations and the profession list and returns it as a DRAFT; a person saves it through the normal create-job endpoint (the UI button does exactly that, so jobs.manage still applies). Agencies get an agency summary, open jobs and roster counts. No tool returns an individual professional's name, contact details, credentials, documents or ratings. The system prompt forbids decisions about eligibility and treats tool results as data, not instructions; clients can send only user/assistant text (max 20 messages, 4,000 characters each), never system or tool content. Rate limit 30 requests per user per hour. The audit log records that a request happened (tool names, token counts, refused/incomplete) and never the conversation. Without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` the assistant reports itself as not switched on and nothing is simulated. Safety refusals become a plain message; provider failures return a generic 503 with no detail. UI: Assistant pages for facilities (with a "Save as a draft job" card) and agencies, with an upfront statement of what it can and cannot do and where data goes. Tests: `backend/tests/assistant.test.ts` (13, using a scripted stand-in for the model, so they run offline and have not exercised the real API). **Not built / not verified:** the real Claude API has never been called from this code (no key was available); no streaming (answers appear when complete); conversations are not saved; no assistant for professionals; no tools that look at individual applicants or candidates; no usage dashboard or spend cap beyond the rate limit.
- Phase 2, workforce analytics (no migration; read-only queries over existing data). Facility: `GET /organizations/:orgId/analytics?from&to` (jobs.read) returns fill rate (positions posted vs filled, overall, by profession and over time), median time to fill, assignment outcomes (completion, no-show and cancellation rates), the application-to-offer funnel (including the share via agencies and offer acceptance), and, only for roles with `billing.read`, cost: approved hours, agreed pay, average rate, platform fees invoiced and paid, by period and profession. Agency: `GET /organizations/:orgId/agency/analytics` (agency.submissions.read) returns submissions, placements, placement rate, approved hours, status mix and top facilities. Periods are UTC calendar days, at most 366, bucketed by week up to 120 days and by month beyond. Figures are aggregates of the organization's own data; no individual is identifiable. UI: Analytics pages for facilities and agencies with stat tiles, grouped columns, ranked bars, hover/keyboard values, legends and a table view of every chart; a small dependency-free chart kit (`apps/web/components/Charts.tsx`). Added a web test setup (`apps/web/vitest.config.ts`, `tests/`) with 7 chart tests including an automated accessibility check, which caught and fixed an unlabeled focus target. Tests: `backend/tests/analytics.test.ts`. **Not built:** platform-wide (admin) analytics, forecasts, exports (CSV/PDF), custom date pickers beyond presets, time zones other than UTC, department/location breakdowns, comparison to a previous period. **Not verified:** the pages have not been looked at in a real browser, so layout and label collisions are unchecked.
- Phase 2, ratings and reliability: migration `0012` (`reviews`, `review_disputes`). Either side can review the other once, only for a completed assignment with an approved timesheet and within 14 days of approval (professionals: `POST /professionals/me/assignments/:id/review`; facilities: `POST /organizations/:orgId/assignments/:id/review`, applications.manage). Reviews are double-blind: neither is visible to the other side until both exist or the window closes, and professionals' reviews are anonymous to the facility. The reviewed party can dispute a visible review once (`POST …/reviews/:id/dispute`); staff with `admin.support` let it stand or remove it with a note (`/admin/reviews/disputes`); a review stays visible and counted while its dispute is open and is never edited. Reliability (completed, no-shows, cancellations, late cancellations within 48 h, completion rate) is computed from assignment history and shown to the professional, and to a facility on that professional's application (`track` on the application detail). Averages are shown to others only from 3 reviews. Professionals see a facility's average on job detail (`organizationRating`). **Ratings and reliability never affect eligibility or ranking.** The facility list is `GET /organizations/:orgId/ratings` because `/reviews` is already the organization approval history. UI: professional Reviews & reliability page, facility Reviews page, admin Review disputes queue, work-record card on the facility application view. Tests: `backend/tests/reviews.test.ts`. **Not built:** editing or deleting your own review, reviewing agencies or agency-submitted work separately, response to a review, abuse/profanity filtering, using reliability in search or notifications.
- Phase 2, timesheets: migration `0011` (`timesheets`, `timesheet_events`). A professional records the hours actually worked for a confirmed or completed assignment once the shift has started (`PUT /professionals/me/assignments/:id/timesheet`, `GET /professionals/me/timesheets`); the facility approves or returns it with a reason (`/organizations/:orgId/timesheets`, `POST …/:id/review`, applications.read / applications.manage). Hours are validated (end after start, not in the future, at most 24 h, break shorter than the work, within 6 h before / 12 h after the scheduled shift). Returned timesheets can be corrected and resubmitted; approved ones are final. Approving a timesheet marks a still-confirmed assignment completed. **Billing now uses approved timesheet hours only** (a completed assignment without an approved timesheet is not billable; the admin preview lists shifts still waiting). Agency placements show timesheet status. UI: professional Timesheets page, facility Timesheets review page, admin billing warnings. Tests: `backend/tests/timesheets.test.ts`; `billing.test.ts` now goes through real timesheets. **Not built:** facility-entered or adjusted hours (a facility can only approve or return), overtime/rate rules, clock-in/out or geolocation, bulk approval, timesheets for permanent hires, agency-side timesheet entry.
- Phase 2, manual invoicing: migration `0010` (`invoices`, `invoice_lines`, invoice number sequence, `billing_updates` notification category). Staff with the new `admin.billing_manage` permission (finance admin, super admin) set the platform fee (`/admin/billing/fee`; **no default, invoices cannot be generated until it is set**), preview and generate draft invoices from completed, unbilled assignments (`/admin/billing/unbilled`, `POST /admin/billing/invoices`), then issue (assigns an `INV-000001` style number, notifies billing contacts), record a manual payment, or void (frees the assignments). Organizations with `billing.read` see only issued invoices (`/organizations/:orgId/billing/invoices[/:id]`). Nothing is charged or collected by the system. UI: admin Billing pages; facility Billing pages for finance contacts. Tests: `backend/tests/billing.test.ts`. **Not built:** payment processor, timesheets (hours are scheduled shift length), agency fees or agency invoices, credit notes, tax, PDF export, overdue reminders.

- Phase 2, staffing agencies (backend only): migration `0009` (`agency_roster`, `job_agency_access`, `applications.submitted_by_agency_id/_user_id`). Agency endpoints under `/organizations/:orgId/agency` (`roster`, `roster/invitations`, `jobs`, `jobs/:jobId/submissions`, `submissions`, `placements`); facility endpoints (`GET /agencies`, `GET|POST /jobs/:jobId/agencies`, `DELETE /jobs/:jobId/agencies/:agencyId`); professional endpoints (`/professionals/me/agencies`, `…/:id/respond`, `DELETE …/:id`).
