# 05 — Facility Portal (Centerpiece of the First Release)

## A. Facility Dashboard

When a facility manager logs in, they immediately see:

- Open positions and unfilled shifts
- New applications and candidate submissions
- Available professionals who may be eligible
- Credential verification status
- Upcoming assignments and staffing gaps
- Pending timesheets and invoices
- Staffing performance and cost summaries

> The dashboard answers one question: **"Do I have the right people to cover my staffing needs?"**

## B. Job and Shift Management

### Permanent Positions
- Job title and specialty
- Required qualifications and experience
- Employment type and compensation range
- Location and schedule
- Application deadline

### Temporary and Per-Diem Shifts
- Shift date and time
- Required professional category and specialty
- Required licenses and certifications
- Number of professionals needed
- Compensation or pay range
- Facility-specific requirements
- Application and confirmation deadlines

**Guardrails:** the platform prevents scheduling conflicts and flags expired or unverified credentials before an assignment is confirmed.

## C. Candidate Discovery and Matching

Facilities can search for professionals by:

- Profession and specialty
- License and credential status
- Geographic location and travel radius
- Availability
- Experience
- Employment preferences
- Compensation expectations
- Previous facility assignments, where permitted

**Matching logic:**
1. Apply mandatory eligibility criteria (hard filters).
2. Rank eligible candidates by relevant preferences (weighted scoring).

> AI can help with candidate discovery and recommendations. It must not replace authoritative credential verification or required human review.

## D. Staffing Agency Integration

Facilities are the primary customer, but they can still invite staffing agencies to submit candidates. A facility can:

1. Post a staffing requirement
2. Search the available professional pool
3. Invite one or more staffing agencies to submit candidates
4. Compare eligible candidates
5. Select a candidate and confirm the assignment
6. Track timesheets and applicable charges

Facilities get this value without BSM having to employ every healthcare professional directly.

## E. Professional Registration (Co-Priority)

The **professional registration experience** shares top priority with the facility portal in the first usable release. It covers profiles, credential uploads, availability and preferences, which together supply the pool facilities search.
